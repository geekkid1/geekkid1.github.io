It's finally time for the last book in the Death saga, _Hogfather_. This is one that I have a bit more familiarity with than most of the other Death saga books. It is by far the one that I've been looking forward to the most in the saga.

I know a bit about the plot, of course. An unhinged member of the Guild of Assassins is commissioned to kill the Hogfather, Discworld's analogue for Santa Claus. Chaos, as ever, ensues. Where does Death come into it? Wait and see.
