Humble Bundle, as of me writing this, has a bundle with 39 of Terry Pratchett's Discworld books for extremely cheap. Discworld has been one of my favorite settings in literature, though I have only read a handful of the books.

Well... now I have a chance to reread those books and more! I intend on reading all 39 of the books I have just gotten my hands on. It's not the _entirety_ of the Discworld collection, but it's more than enough, and it includes the vast majority of the best books from it.

I'm starting with Going Postal, one of my favorites and one of the earliest Discworld memories I have. But keep an eye on my [bookshelf](/books/) collection for those books as I finish and continue chewing through the bundle.
